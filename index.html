I'm Padala Durga Prasadu, a recent B.Tech graduate in Computer Science (AI/ML) from Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education, Tamil Nadu. My work sits at the intersection of neuroscience and artificial intelligence — specifically, building accessible brain-computer interface systems.

Currently pursuing MS admissions in Germany (Winter 2026/27), with a focus on neural engineering and intelligent systems. I believe technology should be built for impact, accessibility, and the long term.
